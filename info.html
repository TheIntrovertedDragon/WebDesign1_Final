Location

Here are some directions to our in-person shop from different roads. Hover to look at the directions, and click to copy them.

From 1-5 North

  1. Take the S. 200TH ST./MILITARY RD. exit, EXIT 151.
  2. Turn RIGHT onto MILITARY RD S.
  3. Turn RIGHT onto S 216TH ST.
  4. S 216TH ST becomes 7TH PL S
  5. Turn LEFT onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509.
  6. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198.

From 1-5 South

  1. Take the S KENT DES MOINES RD/WA-516 W via EXIT 149B toward DES MOINES.
  2. Stay STRAIGHT to go onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509.
  3. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198.

From I-99 (Pacific Highway) North

  1. Take a right onto South 216th ST.
  2. S 216TH ST becomes 7TH PL S.
  3. Turn LEFT onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509.
  4. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198

From I-99 (Pacific Highway) South

  1. Follow 16TH AVE S. North
  2. Turn LEFT onto S KENT DES MOINES RD/WA-516.
  3. At intersection, go ahead through onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509.
  4. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198-6159

From I-509 North

  1. Merge onto WA-509 S toward DES MOINES.
  2. Take the exit on the LEFT toward DES MOINES/S. 188TH ST.
  3. Turn SLIGHT RIGHT onto DES MOINES MEMORIAL DR.
  4. Turn RIGHT to stay on DES MOINES MEMORIAL DR.
  5. Turn LEFT onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509.
  6. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198.

From I-509 South

  1. Starting where PACIFIC HWY S/WA-509/WA-99 are the same roadway,
  2. Turn SLIGHT LEFT onto 16TH AVE S/WA-509. Continue on 16TH AVE S.
  3. Turn LEFT onto S KENT DES MOINES RD/WA-516.
  4. Stay STRAIGHT to go onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509.
  5. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198.

From the Kent-Des Moines Highway (From the East)

  1. Follow W WILLIS ST/WA-516 West
  2. Turn RIGHT onto 24TH AVE S
  3. Turn LEFT onto S 222ND ST
  4. Turn RIGHT onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509
  5. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198

From Des Moines Memorial Drive South

  1. Turn RIGHT to stay on DES MOINES MEMORIAL DR.
  2. Turn LEFT onto MARINE VIEW DR S/WA-509.
  3. End at 21921 Marine View Dr S Des Moines, WA 98198
